What You Get

FIVE WAYS TO
MASTER ESPAÑOL

Each learning mode is powered by Google's Gemini AI and adapts to your CEFR level in real time.

💬
AI Chat Tutor

Have real conversations in Spanish. Gemini replies at your level, gently corrects grammar mistakes, and keeps the conversation going with follow-up questions.

🃏
Smart Flashcards

Generate vocabulary cards on any topic — food, travel, work, and more. Rate each card (Again / Hard / Good) to build your personal word bank with spaced repetition.

📝
Grammar Quiz

Fill-in-the-blank grammar questions generated fresh by Gemini. Get Spanish explanations after each answer so you actually understand why you got it right or wrong.

🎤
Voice Conversation

Speak into your mic in any language. Gemini hears you, thinks, and replies in natural Spanish audio. Real pronunciation practice without a human tutor.

📊
Progress Tracking

Track your day streak, words learned, quiz accuracy, and CEFR level. Auto level-up when your performance earns it. Your progress never resets — it always builds.

🎓
CEFR Levels

Choose A1 through B2. Gemini adjusts vocabulary complexity, sentence length, and correction style to match your exact proficiency level automatically.

Proficiency Levels

YOUR LEVEL,
YOUR PACE

Select your CEFR level and Gemini instantly adapts — simpler sentences for beginners, rich idiomatic Spanish for advanced learners.

A1 Beginner

Basic words, very short sentences, English hints in parentheses. Perfect if you know zero Spanish.

A2 Elementary

Simple everyday phrases, common verbs, occasional English hints for tricky words.

B1 Intermediate

Full Spanish sentences, past and future tenses, gentle grammar corrections. No English hints.

B2 Upper Intermediate

Rich vocabulary, subjunctive mood, idioms. Discusses abstract topics like a native speaker.
